avoid drop shadows and artificial blur entirely. keep everything completely flat.

no cards inside cards. the mother widget has the background color; child widgets inside must be transparent with no extra white or raised card background.

do not wrap numbers or values inside pill badges or box containers. values sit as clean text directly next to labels.

prefer precision geometric outline icons over heavy filled icons or cartoonish glyphs. keep icons inline with text rather than inside circular or square background boxes.

controls and toolbars should flow in a single horizontal line rather than stacking or wrapping into multiple loose rows.

do not split related parameters or status items into multiple separate small cards. instead of 3 or 4 tiny KPI cards, group them inside a single clean container as a unified vertical list with subtle horizontal dividing lines and two-column label/value text alignment.

do not put the icon on its own line or inside a separate icon box above the title. keep the icon and the title inline on the exact same line as a compact header, followed by the description underneath.

for buttons, prefer a minimalist pill shape (rounded-full) that shares the exact same background as the parent card, styled with a soft, subtle thin border and clean dark text, rather than using heavy solid backgrounds or shadows.

when presenting action button pairs, use only these two button styles in a pill shape (rounded-full): a secondary button that has the same background as the card with a thin subtle border, and a primary dark/black button whose font weight is higher (bold/semibold) than the normal one.

for selectable preset or tag collections, prefer an organic pill choice cloud: rounded-full pill chips with a small inline outline icon and label flowing in a wrapping flex layout, preceded by compact pill category filters (active filled dark, inactive subtle outline) and clean two-column pill action buttons at the bottom.

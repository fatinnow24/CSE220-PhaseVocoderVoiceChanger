DSP Mechanics

Time-Shifting Property

When applying Time Stretch, the Phase Vocoder relies on the Fourier transform's time-shifting property. Stretching the magnitude spectrum requires corresponding adjustments to the phase derivatives (instantaneous frequency) to maintain coherence across overlapping frames.

Windowing & Overlap-Add

The signal is multiplied by a Hann window (default) before transformation. To reconstruct the continuous signal without amplitude modulation artifacts, a minimum of 75% overlap is utilized during the inverse transform phase.
